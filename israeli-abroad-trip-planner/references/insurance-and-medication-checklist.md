# Travel insurance interrogation list and medication checklist

Detail for Step 5 of SKILL.md. The body keeps the headline rules; this file carries the full lists so the agent can walk them line by line with a traveler.

## Travel insurance: the lines that actually pay out

Do not ask "do you have travel insurance". Ask these, in order. Each one is a real reason Israeli claims get denied.

| Line to verify | Why it denies claims |
|---|---|
| Bought BEFORE departure | Many policies cannot be issued, or backdated, once the traveler has already left the country. |
| Destination not under an official travel warning | Warning destinations are commonly excluded or surcharged. This is the second consequence of the NSC warning already pulled in Step 2: a destination can be legal to fly to and simultaneously uninsurable. |
| Two-wheelers (קטנוע / אופנוע) | Commonly excluded unless the rider holds a valid motorcycle licence AND buys the extension. Decisive in scooter-heavy destinations. |
| Other activities | Skiing, diving and trekking usually need their own rider. |
| Medical evacuation and repatriation (פינוי רפואי) and its ceiling | The line that matters most in a serious event far from a good hospital. |
| Pre-existing conditions and the health declaration (הצהרת בריאות) | An undisclosed condition can void the whole claim. |
| Pregnancy | Capped by gestational week and often by age; high-risk pregnancy typically excluded. Read the specific cap, never assume. |
| Age | Older travelers face loading or outright refusal. Check price and eligibility before building the trip around them. |
| Trip cancellation and interruption (ביטול או קיצור נסיעה) | For pre-paid flights and hotels. For Israelis, check whether a sudden reserve-duty (miluim) call-up after booking is covered; some policies added this. |
| Maximum trip duration | A policy has a per-trip cap and generally cannot be extended once the traveler is already abroad. Bites the long-stay traveler hardest. |
| Credit-card embedded cover | Usually conditional on having paid for the flight with that card, with narrow day limits and typically no pre-existing-condition cover. A common reason travelers believe they are insured when they are not. Verify rather than assume. |
| The assistance company's phone number | Write down the assistor's number from the policy, not just the insurer's name. In an emergency the traveler calls the assistor, and that is the number they will not find at 03:00. |

## Prescription and controlled medication crossing borders

Ask about regular medication in Step 1, then work this list:

- Carry medication in its ORIGINAL labelled packaging.
- Keep it in HAND luggage, not checked baggage.
- Carry a doctor's letter in English naming the patient, the drug (generic name as well as brand) and the dose.
- Carry only a reasonable quantity for the trip length.
- Plan dosing across time zones before departure.
- Check the destination's own embassy, health authority or customs service for that SPECIFIC drug, well ahead: some destinations require an advance import permit that takes days to weeks.

Highest-risk categories: ADHD stimulants, opioids, benzodiazepines, and cannabis-based products. In some destinations these are controlled or banned outright, and the consequence is refusal of entry or confiscation, not a paperwork delay.

## Where to check

- Ministry of Health, vaccinations for travelers abroad: https://www.gov.il/he/service/vaccination_abroad
- The destination's embassy or consular page for medication and entry rules.
- National Insurance, waiting period after a long stay abroad: https://www.btl.gov.il/Insurance/Living_abroad/Pages/chishuvTkufatHamtana.aspx
