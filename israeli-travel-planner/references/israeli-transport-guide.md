## Rav-Kav Card
- **Personal**: Requires photo, supports period passes
- **Anonymous**: No ID needed, stored value only
- **Reload**: Train stations, bus terminals, post offices, ravkavonline.co.il, ravpass.co.il, or the Rav-Kav Online / HopOn (Rav-Pass) apps (remote NFC loading)
- **Passes**: Daily, weekly, monthly, cluster-based
- **EMV contactless**: Tap a physical credit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay at the validator, no Rav-Kav needed. Not Egged-only: Dan central-Israel buses since October 2022, and an Egged pilot in Eilat, Jerusalem and Haifa in early 2026. Rail acceptance has been announced over several procurement cycles; confirm on rail.co.il before telling a passenger to tap at the gates.

## Public Transport Fares (Derech Shava reform, effective 25 April 2025)
Single-ride bus fares by distance band (bus only):
| Band | Single ride | Daily cap |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Up to 15 km (urban) | 8 NIS | 17.5 NIS |
| Up to 40 km | 14.5 NIS | 29 NIS |
| Up to 75 km | 19 NIS | 37.5 NIS |
| Up to 120 km | 19 NIS | 37.5 NIS |
| Up to 225 km | 30.5 NIS | 60.5 NIS |
| Over 225 km | 74 NIS | 79.5 NIS |

- The up-to-15 km urban ride includes 90 minutes of free transfers in the zone.
- EVERY band has a daily cap, including the over-225 km band. The figures above are the BUS column. Israel Railways has its own higher single-ride and cap column: use the combined-rail figures for any journey that includes a train.
- "Bus" in the official tariff also covers light rail (Dankal, Jerusalem), Metronit, Rakavlit and the Carmelit.
- Nationwide unlimited monthly (bus): 315 NIS.
- Seniors 67+ ride free nationwide.
- Tzedek Tachburti profile discounts: 50% for youth 5-18, women 62-67, qualifying geographic profiles, riders with disabilities and NI benefit recipients; 33% for ages 18-26. Separate profiles exist for children up to age four, soldiers and security forces, and students. The rider must register the profile on their Rav-Kav or app first (online or at an Al HaKav centre) or they pay full fare. No stacking: the system applies the highest single discount. Validation is mandatory on every bus or train boarding, including free riders and pass holders. Source: https://bus.gov.il/Discounts
- Live tariff table: https://bus.gov.il/FaresDistance

## Shabbat Schedule
- Public transit stops: Friday ~2-3 hours before sundown
- Resumes: Saturday ~1 hour after sundown
- Exceptions: Haifa has limited Shabbat bus service
- Alternatives: Sherut taxis and ride/taxi apps (Gett is the main one; verify an app still operates in Israel before naming it, several have exited)
- Municipal and non-state weekend services operate in parts of the country (Haifa's limited municipal service, and various free or low-cost weekend services in central-Israel municipalities and Jerusalem). Coverage is set locally and changes; check the specific origin and destination rather than reciting a list
- Yom Kippur: intercity transit winds down from midday on erev Yom Kippur, the airport and seaports close, and roads are effectively empty by custom. Describe the practical effect, not what the law requires

## National Parks (Rashut HaTeva VeHaGanim)
### Pricing (verify current year at parks.org.il)
- Single entry: Adult ~31-46 NIS at the main tourist sites (Ein Gedi and Banias 31, Masada 37, Caesarea 46, Coral Beach 40), child roughly half; smaller sites lower
- Tourist multi-site cards: Blue (3 sites) 90 NIS, Green (6 sites) 130 NIS, Orange (unlimited) 175 NIS. Valid two weeks from first visit; none covers the Masada cable car
- Masada cable car: separate charge, NOT covered by the tourist cards. Official entrance ~37 NIS adult (21 child, 19 senior); the cable car does not include entrance, at ~32 NIS one-way (16 child) / ~54 NIS round-trip (32 child)
- Matmon annual subscription: INPA does not publish tier prices on its public price list, so quote no figure and point to parks.org.il. Covers entry everywhere except the water sites (Chorshat Tal, Achziv, Gan HaShlosha/Sachne, Palmachim, sea turtle rescue centre), where subscribers pay 50%
- Check the live alerts banner on parks.org.il before recommending a site: per-site closures for heat, flooding, trail damage and safety works are routine
- Hours: Typically 8:00-17:00 (summer until 18:00)

### Top Parks by Region
**North**: Banias, Tel Dan, Gamla, Korazim
**Center**: Caesarea, Beit Guvrin, Apollonia
**South**: Masada, Ein Gedi, Ein Avdat, Timna
**Dead Sea**: Ein Gedi Nature Reserve (advance booking required; flood damage has shortened Nahal David and restricted Nahal Arugot), Qumran

### Israel Railways note
There is no passenger rail to Eilat; Dimona is the southernmost passenger station.

## Driving Tips
- Speed limits: 50 km/h urban, 80-90 km/h rural, 110-120 km/h highway
- Toll roads: Highway 6 (electronic), Carmel Tunnels (cash/electronic)
- Parking: Blue-white curb = paid, red-white = no parking
- Gas prices: ~8.07 NIS/liter for 95-octane self-service (regulated max, May 2026; verify the current monthly cap at gov.il)
