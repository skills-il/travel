# Entry Requirements and Travel Documents for Israeli Travelers

Checked late October 2026 against official sources. Fees are deliberately not hardcoded in SKILL.md because they change (the US ESTA fee changed again on 16 October 2026); read the current fee on the official site.

## Europe (Schengen): ETIAS and EES

- **ETIAS is not operating.** The official EU site states that ETIAS "is currently not in operation and no applications for travel authorisations are collected at this point", and that the EU will announce the start date "several months prior to its launch". No date had been announced as of late October 2026, although the EU's last stated target (April 2026) was the last quarter of 2026. Israel is on the official list of visa-exempt countries whose nationals will need it.
- **After launch:** transitional and grace periods of at least 12 months in total follow, during which travelers without ETIAS are not refused entry if they meet the other conditions. Applications are made only on the official site (europa.eu/etias) or the official app, not before launch. Processing can take up to 30 days in some cases; an approval is valid for three years or until the passport expires.
- **EES** has been fully operational at all external Schengen border crossings since 10 April 2026 (the progressive roll-out began in October 2025). It records biometrics instead of passport stamps. Cyprus and Ireland still stamp passports manually.
- **Passport rule (Schengen Borders Code, Art. 6(1)(a)):** valid at least three months after the intended date of departure from the area, and issued within the previous 10 years.

Sources: https://travel-europe.europa.eu/en/etias , https://travel-europe.europa.eu/en/etias/about-etias/news-corner/revised-timeline-ees-and-etias , https://travel-europe.europa.eu/ees/faq , https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0399

## United Kingdom: ETA

Israeli nationals need an Electronic Travel Authorisation for travel to the UK on or after 8 January 2025. Each traveler needs one, including babies and children, and it is also needed when passing through UK border control in transit. An ETA lasts two years or until the passport expires, and it is linked to the passport used to apply, so a new passport needs a new ETA. A decision usually arrives within a day but can take up to 3 working days. Apply only on gov.uk: other websites may charge more.

Sources: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-eta-national-list , https://www.gov.uk/eta

## United States: ESTA

Israel is in the Visa Waiver Program, so Israeli passport holders travel on an approved ESTA. An e-Passport is required, and CBP recommends applying as soon as travel planning starts or before buying tickets. An authorization is valid for two years or until the passport expires. A new passport requires a new ESTA application. A US citizen who also holds another nationality does not apply for ESTA: CBP expects US citizens to travel to the US on their US passport.

Sources: https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/esta , https://esta.cbp.dhs.gov/

## Canada: eTA

Israelis travelling with a national Israeli passport need an eTA. Holders of an Israeli travel document in lieu of a national passport need a visa instead.

Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/entry-requirements-country.html

## Dual nationals

Ask which passport each traveler will use. CBP states that a US citizen who also holds a Visa Waiver country nationality should not apply for ESTA and is expected to travel to the US on the US passport. gov.uk states that British (and Irish) citizens do not need a UK ETA. For leaving and entering Israel, check the Population and Immigration Authority's current rule for dual citizens: this skill does not state it.

Sources: https://esta.cbp.dhs.gov/assets/i18n/en.json (ESTA FAQ text) , https://www.gov.uk/eta

## Any other country, and transit countries

For any destination not listed above, and for every country a connection passes through, check that country's official immigration site and confirm with the airline before recommending the fare.

## Israeli passport timing

- A biometric passport is issued about 6 weeks after the application, by appointment only.
- If a flight is due before the new passport arrives, the clerk can be asked to leave the old passport valid.
- In the 48 hours before a flight, a temporary passport can be issued only at Ben Gurion airport.
- Adult passports are valid for 10 years; a passport for a minor under 16 is valid for 5 years.

Source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הוצאת_דרכון_ביומטרי

## Travel warnings

The National Security Council publishes travel-warning levels per country; its phone line for travel-warning questions is 02-666-7444, answered 24/7. Check it before booking a cheap destination found through an "anywhere" search.

Source: https://www.gov.il/he/departments/dynamiccollectors/travel-warnings-nsc
