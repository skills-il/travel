# Booking Extras: Deals, Points, Meals

Supporting detail moved out of SKILL.md (body word cap). These do not change the comparison workflow; they are levers to mention when relevant.

## Deal Sources (Israeli)

Error fares and flash deals vanish fast. Israeli travelers also follow deal sites such as secretflights.co.il ("טיסות סודיות") alongside the aggregators. Treat them as a complement, not a replacement, and book fast because error fares get pulled quickly.

## Points and Miles

For frequent flyers the cash price is not the only lever:

- **El Al Matmid** points can be redeemed for award flights; Matmid members also get baggage benefits (see elal.com).
- **Card points**: if the traveler's credit card earns airline points or miles, compare the points cost against the cash fare before booking.

## Kosher and Special Meals

- **El Al** serves only kosher meals, so no special request is needed.
- **Other airlines** (Israeli or foreign): confirm kosher options when booking. On foreign carriers, if you keep kosher, request a kosher meal (special meal code KSML) when booking; each carrier sets its own deadline, and it cannot be arranged at the gate. On very short flights some carriers serve no meal at all.
