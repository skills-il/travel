# TLV Carrier Status (snapshot, late October 2026)

Ben Gurion has been fully open since 9 April 2026 and operates normally (it partially reopened for Israeli airlines in early March, after the war began on 28 February). Carrier status after the February 2026 war still moves week to week, so this file is a dated snapshot for orientation only. Before recommending any route, confirm it on the airline's own site or in a live search.

## Israeli carriers to the Gulf

El Al, Israir and Arkia have not flown to the UAE since security authorities halted those flights at the start of the February 2026 war. After the flydubai suspension they asked to resume; El Al said it plans at least two daily Dubai flights from November 2026, subject to state approval. Treat Dubai as unavailable on Israeli carriers until a resumption is confirmed.
