# Israeli Airlines Baggage Quick Reference

Quick-reference table for baggage policies of airlines operating from Ben Gurion (TLV).
El Al, Israir and Wizz Air verified October 2026 on the airlines' own pages. Arkia is not listed: arkia.co.il blocks automated reading, so read its allowance and fees for the route in Arkia's booking flow. Always verify on the airline's website before booking.

**Currency warning:** Israir and Wizz prices are in USD/EUR. Before adding any of them to a shekel comparison, convert with an exchange rate read this session, and add the ~3% Israeli card foreign-currency fee only to amounts the card is actually charged in foreign currency. Never convert from memory.

## Key Differences

- **El Al** is the most generous with baggage on Classic fares and above
- **Arkia**: read the allowance and fees live for the specific route and fare
- **Israir** charges for everything except a personal item on standard fares, except on New York flights, where a carry-on and checked bags are included
- **Wizz Air** charges for everything beyond a small personal item on base fares
- **El Al and Israir** charge significantly more for baggage at the airport than online in advance
