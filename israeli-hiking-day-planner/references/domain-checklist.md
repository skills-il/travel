# Domain checklist: israeli-hiking-day-planner

Research date: 2026-10-23. Evidence ids in brackets refer to `evidence-draft.json`.

## Must cover (core)

- [ ] IMS heat-stress scale - Six categories from the discomfort index HSI = (T + Tw)/2: none <22.0, light (קל) 22.0-23.9, mild (מתון) 24.0-25.9, moderate (בינוני) 26.0-27.9, heavy (כבד) 28.0-29.9, extreme (קיצוני) >=30.0 [ims-heat-*] - source: https://ims.gov.il/sites/default/files/2025-01/%D7%9E%D7%92%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9D%20%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%95%D7%AA_29122024.pdf - why core: INPA and MoE rules are keyed to these names ("בינוני", "כבד"), so the go/no-go logic needs the exact bands.
- [ ] Reading heat stress from the ims-weather MCP - `get_forecast_data(location_id)` returns 7 days of hourly `heat_stress` (numeric) and `heat_stress_level` (integer). Compute the band from the numeric `heat_stress` using the IMS colorbar (22/24/26/28/30). Do NOT trust `heat_stress_level`: 28.4 comes back as 3 [ims-forecast-hourly-heat-stress-field, ims-heat-stress-level-field-mismatch, ims-api-colorbar-heat-thresholds] - source: https://ims.gov.il/he/forecast_data/80 and https://ims.gov.il/he/city_portal/80 - why core: this is the only automated heat input the skill has.
- [ ] Hourly heat profile, not the daily max - The heat band changes through the day (Ein Gedi 23/10/2026: light at 08:00, heavy (28.4) at 15:00). The skill must pick the hours the hike will actually take [ims-forecast-hourly-heat-stress-field] - source: https://ims.gov.il/he/forecast_data/80 - why core: start/finish time is chosen against the hourly curve.
- [ ] IMS alerts check - `get_ims_alerts` returns live warnings with `warning_type_id` (8 heat stress, 5 floods, 7 flooding, 11 thunderstorms, 10 fire danger, 33 heat wave), `severity_id` (2 yellow early warning, 3 yellow, 4 orange, 5 orange early, 6 red early, 7 red), region ids and validity window [ims-warning-types-include-heat-flood, ims-warning-severities, ims-live-heat-warning-2026-10-23] - source: https://ims.gov.il/he/ims_alerts - why core: a flood or heat warning for the route region is an automatic no-go or a reroute.
- [ ] INPA heat rules for specific sites (Masada) - Snake Path: moderate heat, last ascent 10:00 / last descent 11:00; heavy heat, closes 08:00 ascent / 09:00 descent; summer extreme, Snake 07:00/08:00, Ramp 11:00/12:00, Runner's path and Ma'ale Elazar 07:00. INPA says it works by the IMS forecast [inpa-masada-*] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/reserve-park/%D7%92%D7%9F-%D7%9C%D7%90%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%93%D7%94/ - why core: the only INPA page found that ties hours to named IMS heat bands.
- [ ] INPA site hours and last entry (Ein Gedi / Nahal Arugot) - Pre-registration required; Hidden Waterfall last entry 13:00, leave waterfall by 14:30, reserve by 16:00 (winter; +1 h in summer time); upper pools ascent by 12:30; entry up to 3 h before closing; 3 L (waterfall) / at least 4 L (upper pools) per person [inpa-arugot-*] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/reserve-park/arugot/ - why core: last-entry hours are hard constraints on the start time.
- [ ] Water per person (civilian) - INPA: 3 L per person in the north, 5 L in the south [inpa-water-3l-north-5l-south]; site-specific figures override (Arugot 3 L / 4 L) - source: https://www.parks.org.il/new/beticut/ - why core: the water line on the route card must cite this, not a blog figure.
- [ ] Water per hour (school rule, for scaling) - MoE info card: ages 6-10 half a liter per walking hour, 11-18 three quarters of a liter per walking hour; drink about a third of a liter every half hour [moe-card-*] - source: https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/noar/heat_load.pdf - why core: the only official per-hour figure; label it as a Ministry of Education school guideline, not a civilian rule.
- [ ] Start early, finish before dark - INPA: start early morning, finish before dark, walking in darkness is forbidden; if caught by dark, stop, stay on the trail, call for help. MoE (schools): finish about an hour before dark [inpa-start-early-finish-before-dark, inpa-caught-by-dark-stop, moe-finish-hour-before-dark] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/new/beticut/ , https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Bitachon/teyulim2016.pdf - why core: drives the latest start time computation.
- [ ] Sunset time source - IMS API exposes no sunrise/sunset; use Hebcal zmanim API by lat/lng (`sunset`), and account for the DST change (Israel switches to winter time in late October) [hebcal MCP] - source: the hebcal MCP (Hebcal zmanim API) - why core: finish-time math. Note Hebcal is not a government source; say so.
- [ ] Hot hours to avoid - MoE youth booklet: avoid exertion 10:00-16:00 in heat; MoE card: aim to stop between 10 and 4 in the hot hours [moe-youth-avoid-10-16, moe-card-stop-10-4] - source: https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Bitachon/youth-movements-outdoor-activities-and-trips-booklet.pdf - why core: start-time window on hot days.
- [ ] Heavy heat = no hike (school rule) and INPA "skip sharav days" - MoE: heavy heat forecast the evening before at destination means no field trips; heavy heat all day with no hour limit means no activity. INPA: hiking forbidden in flood-danger areas or on sharav days; give up the hike on sharav or flood days [moe-no-field-trips-heavy-heat, moe-heavy-heat-all-day-no-activity, inpa-no-hiking-flood-sharav, inpa-skip-sharav-flood-days] - source: https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Bitachon/teyulim2016.pdf , https://www.parks.org.il/article/plan-your-trip/ - why core: the no-go threshold.
- [ ] Flash floods: rain upstream - MoE: in the rainy season check whether rain is expected upstream before entering a wadi; never cross a flowing wadi on foot or by vehicle; avoid narrow canyons when rain is forecast that day. Kumran card: rain at a high point drains into a canyon where no rain fell and the sun shines [moe-wadi-upstream-rain, moe-no-crossing-flood, moe-avoid-narrow-canyons-rain-alert, moe-youth-hidden-rain-upstream] - source: https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Bitachon/teyulim2016.pdf - why core: the main killer in desert canyons.
- [ ] Ein Gedi 2025 flood as the worked example - IMS: over 30 mm in 10 minutes; Arugot above 200 m3/s while no rain fell on more than 90% of its catchment [ims-ein-gedi-flood-2025-30mm] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/new/arugot/ - why core: proves "sunny where you are" is not safe.
- [ ] INPA closures and newsflashes - Check the INPA closed-trails page (updated 11/10/2026) and newsflashes before every hike [inpa-closed-trails-page-2026-10-11, inpa-check-newsflashes] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/newsflash/maslulim-2/ - why core: no MCP covers INPA status, the skill must route the user there.
- [ ] Mine danger in the north - Absolute ban on leaving marked trails in listed reserves (Snir-Hatzbani, Yehudiya, Meshushim, Hermon-Banias, Tel Dan, and more) [inpa-mine-danger-golan-north]; MoE: do not enter areas with yellow signs or red triangles [moe-mines-yellow-signs] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/newsflash/maslulim-2/ - why core: off-trail shortcuts in the Golan kill.
- [ ] Unexploded ordnance (war) - INPA 09/04/2026: munitions may be present; do not touch, call police 100; HFC home page 22/10/26 repeats it [inpa-uxo-call-100] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/newsflash/%d7%a4%d7%99%d7%a7%d7%95%d7%93-%d7%94%d7%a2%d7%95%d7%a8%d7%a3-%d7%99%d7%95%d7%a6%d7%90%d7%99%d7%9d-%d7%9c%d7%98%d7%99%d7%99%d7%9c-%d7%94%d7%99%d7%96%d7%94%d7%a8%d7%95-%d7%9e%d7%a0%d7%a4%d7%9c%d7%99/ - why core: current wartime hazard on trails.
- [ ] IDF firing zones - Entry to a closed area requires a written permit (Defence Regulations 1945, reg. 125); civilians coordinate through the regional command's מתא"ם; the State Comptroller (2021) found no coordination info on the IDF website; Southern Command says coordination is possible only until Shabbat or the holiday begins, so an uncoordinated weekend route is a no-go [mevaker-reg125-written-permit, mevaker-coordination-via-matam, idf-south-coordination-only-before-shabbat] - source: https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2021/71B-Bitachon/2021-71B-403-idf-training.pdf - why core: the skill must say "coordinate first, weekends included" and must NOT print a phone number (none verified on an official page in 2026).
- [ ] Home Front Command status - As of 22/10/26 the defense policy reads "no restrictions nationwide"; the map excludes per-locality exceptions; "gathering" = more than 10 people [oref-*] - source: https://www.oref.org.il/heb/articles/info/iron-swords/1100 - why core: a restriction in the hike area is a no-go; status changes daily, so check on the day.
- [ ] Emergency: who to call - INPA: in distress call police 100 (or ZAKA 1220); UXO also 100 [inpa-distress-call-100]. What to say: exact location, what happened, what help is needed (MoE guidance) [moe-youth-distress-call-contents]; detached hiker sits and waits on the trail [moe-detached-sit-and-wait] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/article/safe-trip/ - why core: emergency plan on the route card.
- [ ] Leave a trip plan - INPA: leave a written note at INPA sites or on the car window (details, phone, route, group size, schedule) and tell relatives [inpa-leave-trip-note] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/new/beticut/ - why core: there is no free official civilian hike-registration service (see Out of scope).
- [ ] Marked trails only - INPA: walking and driving only on marked trails [inpa-marked-trails-only]; use an updated trail-marking map - source: https://www.parks.org.il/new/beticut/ - why core: OSM routes from plan_route can follow unmarked or closed paths.
- [ ] What the hiking MCP can and cannot do - 5 tools: search_pois, plan_route, find_trails, get_poi_details, convert_coordinates. plan_route returns distance, elevation (ascent/descent/min/max), road types; NO duration, NO difficulty, NO closure status. find_trails is a name search, not a radius search [hiking-mcp-*] - source: https://github.com/skills-il/mcps/tree/master/israel-hiking-mcp - why core: the skill must compute duration itself and must say difficulty is an estimate.
- [ ] IMS location coverage - IMS locations include INPA sites (80 Ein Gedi, 267 Masada, 261 Qumran, 255 Ein Prat, 211 Nahal Amud, 704 Ramon) [ims-locations-include-reserves] - source: https://ims.gov.il/he/locations_info - why core: pick the nearest IMS location to the trailhead, not the nearest city.
- [ ] Fire ban - Lighting fire in nature reserves and national parks is strictly forbidden; at Arugot including gas stoves and cigarettes [inpa-no-fire-in-reserves, inpa-arugot-no-fire-incl-stoves] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/article/plan-your-trip/ - why core: packing list and route-card rules.

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)

- [ ] Swimming / water entry - INPA: jumping into water is dangerous and forbidden [inpa-jumping-into-water-forbidden]; MoE school rule: bathing in pits and pools in streams strictly forbidden [moe-pools-swimming-banned] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/new/beticut/ - why: kids and pools are a common plan item.
- [ ] Stream water quality - Ministry of Health recommendation 400 fecal coliforms per 100 ml; in October 2026 INPA reported several northern streams above it [inpa-water-quality-400-fecal] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/newsflash/%d7%9e%d7%a9%d7%a8%d7%93-%d7%94%d7%91%d7%a8%d7%99%d7%90%d7%95%d7%aa-%d7%95%d7%94%d7%9e%d7%a9%d7%a8%d7%93-%d7%9c%d7%94%d7%92%d7%a0%d7%aa-%d7%94%d7%a1%d7%91%d7%99%d7%91%d7%94-%d7%9e%d7%95%d7%93%d7%99-2/ - why: wet-trail planning in the north.
- [ ] Never drink untreated natural water - MoE: drinking from field taps, fire hydrants, pits, springs, stream beds not approved by the Ministry of Health is forbidden - source: https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Bitachon/teyulim2016.pdf - why: water resupply planning.
- [ ] Afternoon wadi heat and first-day acclimatization - MoE card: summer afternoons wadi floors re-radiate heat (Judean Desert, Negev); most heat events on the first day in the field [moe-card-afternoon-wadi-heat, moe-card-first-day-acclimatization] - source: https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/noar/heat_load.pdf - why: tourists and first-time desert hikers.
- [ ] Fluid loss rate - MoE card: vigorous activity on a hot day loses 1 to 1.5 L per hour [moe-card-fluid-loss-1-1.5l-hour] - source: https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/noar/heat_load.pdf - why: explains why 5 L in the south is a floor.
- [ ] Long field-journey water table (school rule) - MoE youth booklet table, high-school long journeys: normal heat 2h 1.5 L / 4h 3 L / 6h 4 L / 8h 4.5 L / 10h 6 L; heavy heat 1.5 / 3 / 4.5 / 6 / 6 [moe-youth-water-table, confidence medium on column alignment] - source: https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Bitachon/youth-movements-outdoor-activities-and-trips-booklet.pdf - why: long-day hikes; label as school guideline.
- [ ] Emergency water in vehicle (desert) - MoE: 60 L emergency water in the vehicle on desert trips, not counted as drinking water [moe-desert-60l-emergency] - source: https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Bitachon/teyulim2016.pdf - why: car-based desert day trips (scale down for a family, cite as school rule).
- [ ] Israel National Trail marking - White, blue and orange, marked by SPNI [spni-int-marking-colors] - source: https://www.teva.org.il/israel-national-trail - why: route card legend.
- [ ] MoE trips situation room - 02-6222211, 24 h, operated via Moked Teva, serves Education-system groups; SPNI Moked Teva 24/7 is subscribers-only [moe-situation-room-phone, spni-moked-teva-subscribers-only] - source: https://www.teva.org.il/moked-teva - why: group leaders (youth movements, schools) using the skill must use it; private hikers cannot.
- [ ] Ein Gedi ascents closed in heat - INPA closed all ascents at Ein Gedi "until September" in 2025 because of heat stress [inpa-engedi-ascents-closed-heat] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/new/arugot/ - why: shows INPA seasonal heat closures exist but are announced per season, not as a standing table.
- [ ] Northern-border closures by IDF on the INPA list - INPA list includes IDF-closed segments (e.g., Har HaNegev Ma'ale Horsha crossing to Route 10) and Golan Trail segment 4 closed for demining Sun-Thu 05:45-16:30 until 15.12.2026 - source: https://www.parks.org.il/newsflash/maslulim-2/ - why: current-status example.
- [ ] Summer/winter-time hours shift - INPA hours lengthen by one hour in summer time; Israel switches to winter time in late October, so hours for a date after the switch use the winter table [inpa-arugot-winter-hours, inpa-arugot-summer-plus-1h] - source: https://www.parks.org.il/reserve-park/arugot/ - why: off-by-one-hour last-entry errors this week.
- [ ] HFC group size - Under any HFC restriction, a "gathering" is more than 10 people; a group hike of 11+ may be affected [oref-gathering-over-10] - source: https://www.oref.org.il/heb/articles/info/iron-swords/1100 - why: groups.

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)

- Technical canyoning / rappelling (סנפלינג) routes - INPA runs a separate general permit regime with listed sites and seasons; a day-hike planner should refuse to plan rope descents and point to that permit page.
- Multi-day treks and overnight camping - night-camp rules (חניוני לילה) and night water management are different; the youth booklet covers them but they are not a day hike.
- Area A in Judea and Samaria - entry by Israeli citizens is prohibited by law and dangerous, per police statements reported in the press. I could not verify the official sign wording or an official police/IDF page in this pass. The skill should refuse to route into Area A and must not paraphrase a "sign text". Hikes elsewhere in Judea and Samaria: the MoE requires security coordination for schools; no official civilian coordination requirement was found.
- Medical treatment of heat stroke beyond first-aid triage - the MoE symptoms table is a CDC/NIOSH reprint; the skill should tell users to call 100/101, not treat.
- Off-road 4x4 driving - plan_route supports a 4WD profile, but INPA 4x4 rules are separate.
- Hike registration services - SPNI Moked Teva is subscribers-only and the MoE situation room serves Education-system groups. No free official civilian "register your hike" service was found in 2026. The skill should not promise one; it should give the INPA "leave a note" rule instead.

## Known bad figures

- "Water is 750 cc per child per walking hour plus a third of a liter per riding hour, with a break at least once an hour" - from an older MoE circular (archive `nh10bk7_11_14.htm`, surfaced by a search-engine summary only; I did not open that page, so treat even the attribution as unverified). The current MoE heat card says half a liter (ages 6-10) / three quarters of a liter (11-18) per walking hour, and a short break every half hour. Do not use the 750 cc figure.
- MoE heat card refers readers to "חוזר מנכ"ל לטיולים תשס"ה 9/109(ג)", a 2005 circular. The 2016 standing orders (עו/7(ב)) replaced it and give different water numbers (1.5 L / 3 L / 4.5 L minimum per day on foot, not per hour). The two MoE documents measure different things (daily minimum vs per-hour rate); quote each with its unit.
- "Arugot: pools until 14:00, waterfall until 15:00 (winter)" (tiuli.com) and "entry until two and a half hours before closing" (srugim) - both contradict the current INPA page (leave the Hidden Waterfall by 14:30; entry up to three hours before closing; last entry 13:00).
- Southern Command firing-zone phone numbers 08-9902926/8 (news, 2023) and 08-9902927 (shvilim.co.il) - not on any official IDF page; the State Comptroller found a third-party listing of the מתא"ם South number was out of date. Do not print any of them.
- "Firing zones are open on weekends without coordination" - wrong: reg. 125 requires a written permit for a closed area, and Southern Command (reported by Srugim, 21/05/2026) says coordination is possible only until Shabbat or the holiday begins. (The State Comptroller report quotes this phrase from a tour company page; it is not the Comptroller's own finding.)
- IMS API `heat_stress_level` integer - does not match the published scale (28.4 heavy is returned as level 3). Treat as an undocumented field.
- "Trail color indicates difficulty" - common belief; only a Hebrew encyclopedia (secondary) says colors are identifiers only. Not verified on an SPNI/Trails Committee page, so the skill should say nothing about color meaning beyond "the color identifies the trail on the map" if it can cite it, or omit it.
